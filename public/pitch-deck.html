01 · Solving Fragmented Intelligence

Sponsors in product

Zetaris · Meterless World Model · Nightshift (Nemotron) · Bright Data · Cursor

Not legal advice

Problem

UK redress is fragmented. Citizens pay for that.

  • After a Final Response, deadlock, or DPO reply, people guess the door: FOS, Energy Ombudsman, ICO, parking ADR, FSCS.
  • Wrong door or filing too early/late burns the limitation clock and months of effort.
  • Chatbots that invent dates or soft-pedal blockers make it worse.

Design principle

Deterministic gates decide Eligible / Premature / Out of Time / Wrong Door. AI extracts facts — it does not invent clocks.

Slide 2 / 7

Users

Who this is for

Primary

Unrepresented UK consumers with a firm’s written reply — Final Response, energy deadlock, DPO final position, or parking rejection.

Not for

Claims management companies, solicitors running volume intake, or anyone expecting auto-submit to a scheme portal.

  • Citizen stays in control: approve pack sections I–IV, then file on the official portal themselves.
  • Plain-English stories + ⓘ tips — “Did a statutory door open?” — not legalese first.

Slide 3 / 7

Solution

One path: letter → extract → gate → pack

1 IntakePaste / PDF / image. As-of date for clocks.
2 ExtractNightshift (Nemotron) reads firm, letter type, dates.
3 GateCode-only: Eligible, Premature, Out of Time, Wrong Door.
4 PackHuman approves I–IV → .md + .pdf → file on portal.
  • Verified · Missing · Blocker on every fact — missing clock dates block continue.
  • ICO Special only when routed to ICO; skipped for FOS / Energy.
  • Export locked until every section is approved.
